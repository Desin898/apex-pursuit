APEX PURSUIT — PHASE 4D.1 PROMPT: MY OWN MUSIC IN THE GAME (FC-STYLE MUSIC MENU)
================================================================================

Why
---
The Spotify version is not what I want:
- it needs Spotify in the browser, it plays 30-second previews unless signed in, and it feels laggy;
- on the claude.ai page it's blocked;
- the Spotify card on the event screen covers the "Start race" button, so I can't start the race.

What I want instead: the game plays MY music files (Apex_Playlist.zip, 14 MP3 tracks, ~130 MB),
with a music menu like EA SPORTS FC 26, and no Spotify anywhere.

1. Remove Spotify completely
   - No Spotify script, player, card, link or "previews" note. Delete the Spotify playlist list.
   - Nothing may cover menu buttons ("Start race" etc.) on any screen size.

2. The game plays my own audio files
   - Tracks play through the game's own audio engine (Web Audio): no lag, no extra services.
   - Loading the music: "Music → Add music": pick the MP3 files or the whole Apex_Playlist folder
     (or drag them onto the window). The game keeps them in the browser (IndexedDB), so it's a
     one-time step per browser; works in the downloaded game and on the claude.ai page.
   - Read the title, artist and cover art from each file's tags (ID3), falling back to the file
     name ("Artist - Title"), and clean names ("(SPOTISAVER)" removed, "#U00ae" shown as ®).
   - The music files are mine: they must NOT be committed to the public GitHub repo or put in
     the published claude.ai page. (.gitignore any music folder.) Friends add their own music the
     same way.
   - Optional: a personal build for me with the 14 tracks already inside, delivered only to me.

3. "Now playing" on the home screen, not distracting
   - A small one-line ticker: "♪ Now playing · Title — Artist", placed where it never covers a
     button or the race list (e.g. top-right corner of the home screen), fading after a few
     seconds and showing again when the track changes.
   - In a race: the same small line appears for ~5 s when a track starts, then hides.
   - Press a button (e.g. ▲ / Y / Tab) on the home screen to expand it into the full Music menu.

4. Music menu, like FC 26 (see the attached pictures)
   - Reached from the home screen's settings area ("Music") and from the expanded ticker.
   - Left: a grid of tracks with cover art, title and artist; a ✓ on each track that is in the
     rotation (toggle per track); "Turn all on / off".
   - Right: the selected track large (cover, title, artist), and "Currently playing" with a
     progress bar and time.
   - Controls: ✕ / Enter = play this track now; ○ / Esc = back; □ = turn all on/off;
     △ = play / stop; L3 + R3 (or M) = skip to the next track from any screen.
   - After a chosen track ends, the rotation continues as usual (shuffled through the tracks
     that are ticked), looping forever.
   - "Music always on: on/off" (music in races or only on menus), and the Music / game balance,
     Engine, Effects and Sirens volumes stay (now the music volume is really controllable).
   - Works with mouse, keyboard and DualShock 4.

5. Checks
   - No overlap with any button at 640×360 up to 2560×1440 (hudcheck).
   - Music keeps playing across menus → race → results without gaps or double playback.
   - Online and solo unaffected; performance unaffected (audio decoding off the main thread
     where possible, one track loaded at a time).
   - Commit to github.com/Desin898/apex-pursuit (code only), refresh play/ApexPursuit.html,
     publish the claude.ai page, report what changed and what's not done.
