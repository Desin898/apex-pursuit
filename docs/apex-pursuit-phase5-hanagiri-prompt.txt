APEX PURSUIT — Phase 5: Hanagiri Pass (the mountain venue)

Goal
Add the second venue, Hanagiri Pass: a Japanese mountain road with real height, tight
hairpins, guard rails and cherry blossom, raced as a full circuit, as point-to-point sprints
and as 1-vs-1 touge battles. The Proving Grounds stays exactly as it is.

1. The road and its height
- A closed loop of about 6-7 km climbing about 180 m: a fast valley straight, an uphill
  section of esses, three or four hairpins, a summit ridge with a view, then a long downhill
  with a tunnel and a river bridge back to the start village.
- Every road sample gets a height (y). heightAt(x, z) returns the road surface on the road
  and the slope beside it, so the car's wheels, the AI and the camera follow it.
- Physics: a slope term (gravity along the road), so uphill slows you and downhill pulls.
  Only venues with height use it, so the Proving Grounds' lap times don't change.
- Road, kerbs, lines, shoulders and run-off all follow the height; a camber on corners.
- The physics test gets new checks: a climb, a descent and a hairpin at speed.

2. Terrain and world around it
- Venue-specific terrain: the mountain the road is cut into, cliffs above, drops below,
  distant ranges, a valley with a river. Bounds and the far shadow cover the new area.
- Guard rails (steel, with posts) on every drop; retaining walls and rock cuts on the
  mountain side; a tunnel (lit at night); the river bridge.
- Dressing: cherry blossom trees and falling petals, cedar forest, a shrine gate and stone
  lanterns, a small village at the start (wooden houses, vending machine lights, invented
  shop signs only), rice terraces in the valley, spectators at the hairpins, a car park at
  the summit, snow poles and road signs (invented, no real brands).
- Weather and time of day work as at the Proving Grounds (fog in the valley is welcome).

3. Racing at Hanagiri
- Circuit race: laps of the loop (8 cars), as today.
- Sprint: start village → summit (uphill) or summit → village (downhill); finish by
  distance, not laps.
- Touge battle: 1 vs 1, two runs, leader and chaser swap; gap at the line decides it.
- Pursuit works too (police from the village and the summit car park).
- Online: the host's venue and mode are sent to everyone, as in 4D.
- Event card: the Venue row offers Hanagiri Pass (no longer "coming in Phase 5"), with
  the new modes; the track map shows height as shading.

4. Checks and delivery
- Physics test passes (old + new checks); HUD check passes; net test passes.
- Before/after pictures in docs/5/, F4 benchmark numbers for both venues.
- Draw calls at Hanagiri within about 10% of the Proving Grounds at the same quality.
- Commit and push the code; refresh play/ApexPursuit.html; publish the artifact (it keeps
  your music); source zip; README "New in Phase 5"; report what's done and what isn't.
