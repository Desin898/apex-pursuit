APEX PURSUIT — Phase 4E: "Air One", a live police helicopter (+ Proving Grounds fixes)

Reference images (attached): a helicopter low over a dusk pursuit with its searchlight on
the road; a patrol helicopter close up (shape, skids, nose camera ball); a helicopter
over a wet city street at night, lit by its own spotlight; a helicopter tilted hard over
a chase with police cars behind. Use them for mood, lighting and motion only. Every
marking stays invented: no real police department, registration, car maker or plate.

Today Air One only shows up at Heat 5, which a 3-lap race rarely reaches, and it mostly
hovers. This phase makes it a real part of the chase you can see, hear and fight.

1. Looks and feel (the helicopter itself)
- A more detailed original model: nose camera / searchlight ball, sliding-door outline,
  window frames, antennas, step bars on the skids, exhaust, tail fin decal, an invented
  livery ("AIR ONE", unit number, made-up city crest). Close up it should hold up like
  the close-up reference.
- It flies like a helicopter: nose dips to speed up, it banks into turns, flares to slow,
  the tail sways; small wobble in wind and rain.
- Rotor blades that turn, blur into a disc at speed, and catch the light (no flicker).
- Lights: red/blue light bar, white strobe, red/green navigation lights, landing light.
- A real searchlight: a beam you can see in rain and fog that actually lights the road,
  the cars, the barriers and the trees where it points (one moving light, with a soft edge
  and a slight shadow if it's cheap enough). It tracks you, sweeps when it loses you.
- Rotor wash under it: rain blown sideways, spray and dust kicked off the road, grass and
  trees bending, smoke pushed away.
- Sound: a deep rotor thump you feel before you see it, louder and lower as it passes
  overhead (Doppler), turbine whine, radio chatter lines.

2. When it comes and how it chases
- Joins at Heat 3 (Settings: Air support = From Heat 3 / From Heat 5 / Off).
- Flies in from the horizon with a radio call ("Air One on scene") and stays with you:
  ahead of you on straights, swinging wide in corners, sometimes low and close over the
  road like the reference shots, never clipping the stands, towers or wires.
- It tells the ground units where you are while it can see you. Breaking its line of
  sight (under the grandstand roof, through the garages, under trees, in heavy rain/fog)
  starts the Evade timer like losing a patrol car does.
- It has limited fuel: after a while it leaves ("Air One returning to refuel") and comes
  back later at high Heat.

3. What it drops (arcade, always warned, always dodgeable)
- Smoke canisters: they bounce on the road and pour out thick grey/red smoke that hangs
  across the track for a few seconds and hides the corner ahead. Driving through rocks the
  camera a little and leaves a trail of smoke behind you. No damage.
- Spike strips: dropped across your lane a few seconds ahead; a red marker on the road and
  the minimap shows where. Hit them = a puncture: less grip and top speed until the end of
  the lap (or a pit stop later), sparks from the rim.
- Shock charges (mini bombs): small charges land with a blinking red ring on the road; one
  second later a bang, a flash, dust and sparks; a car inside the ring gets shoved and spun,
  loses speed, camera shake and controller rumble. They never hit police cars by design
  (the pilot avoids its own units) and never land without the warning ring.
- How often and how many grows with Heat; Settings can turn the drops off and keep only the
  searchlight helicopter.

4. Game side
- HUD: helicopter icon on the minimap with its light cone, an "AIR ONE" tag when it's
  chasing you, the warning ring/markers, and a "Spotted / Hidden from Air One" note.
- New camera: Air One cam (looking down from the helicopter) for photo mode and as a short
  cut-in when it arrives.
- Online: the host flies the helicopter and decides the drops; everyone sees the same
  smoke, spikes and bangs at the same places.
- AI rivals react to smoke and markers (slow down, swerve).

5. Proving Grounds fixes left from 4D
- Gravel traps drive like gravel: lower grip, strong drag, the car wobbles, stones spray
  and rattle, dust trail, gravel sound; AI avoids them.
- Floodlights really light the track at dusk and night: pools of light on the road and kerbs
  under each tower, done in the road shader so it stays fast.
- Fixed already: Back / Esc in the race pause menu sometimes jumped to the home screen.

6. Checks and delivery

- Frame-rate cost: helicopter + searchlight + drops add no more than about 1-2 ms on High
