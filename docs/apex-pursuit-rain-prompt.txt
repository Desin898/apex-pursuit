GOAL
Rain that looks like the reference photos instead of a particle effect. Water lives in the world (in the air, on the road, thrown up by the tyres), not as bubbles stuck to the camera.

1. NO BUBBLES ON THE CAMERA
- Lens drops in the chase, hood and bumper views are OFF by default.
- Add a "Lens drops" setting: Off / Light.
  - Light means a handful of small, short-lived drops.
  - No sideways running.
  - Only a gentle refraction, with no visible rim.
- Remove the "run sideways at speed" behaviour completely.
- In the cockpit, water stays on the windscreen (see 6), never on the camera.

2. RAIN IN THE AIR
- Short, thin streaks (about 15–40 cm), not long lines.
- Cap the streak length at speed so they never fan out from the centre of the screen.
- Fade streaks within about 2.5 m of the camera.
- Light the rain:
  - A drop is nearly invisible in the dark and bright where it passes through a street lamp, headlight or police light.
  - Use the same light list the smoke uses.
- Give the falling rain a little wind drift, and vary the density in gusts.
- Add a distant rain haze: a faint, light-catching mist that thickens the fog with distance in the rain and glows around lamps.

3. SPRAY FROM EVERY CAR
- Each car on a wet road throws a rooster tail behind each rear tyre:
  - It grows with speed and hangs in the air for 1–2 s.
  - It is lit by that car's tail lights (red glow inside the spray) and by any nearby lamps.
- Thinner mist off the front tyres.
- This applies to rivals and police, not just the player.
- Driving into another car's spray briefly cuts how far you can see.
- Budget the particle count per quality preset.

4. THE WET ROAD
- Stretch the reflections of lights vertically (anisotropic streaks down the road), the way wet tarmac smears light.
- Red tail lights and police strobes reflect as long glowing streaks.
- Add puddles that ripple, and small splashes where drops hit the road near the camera.
- Keep the Phase 3D planar mirror, with roughness so it isn't perfectly sharp.

5. LIGHTS IN THE RAIN
- Street lamps, headlights and police lights get a soft glow halo in the rain, and their beams show in the mist on High and Ultra.

6. COCKPIT WINDSCREEN (Image 3)
- Small beads plus a few larger drops that slide up the glass at speed.
- The wiper clears an arc and the water builds back up behind it.
- Lights seen through the wet glass blur into soft blobs.

7. QUALITY BAR
- Rain costs:
  - at most about 1 ms on High on an RTX 3050 laptop
  - at most 0.5 ms on Medium
  - on Low, only the streaks and simple spray
- Compare before and after at the same spots:
  - the chase camera on the start straight in rain at night
  - behind a rival at speed
  - the cockpit in rain
- Update README with the new settings and tunables.

Start with sections 1 to 3. Then do 4 to 6, publish a playable build, and tell me what changed and what is not done.
